## Missing Content Strategy

**Decision**: Option A -- Drill prescriptions for techniques without approved videos.

When no approved video exists for a technique, Musashi provides a text-based drill description:

> "I don't have a video on that one yet, but here's a drill to try:
> [Step 1]
> [Step 2]
> [Step 3]
> Give it 5 reps at the start of your next session."

This keeps the experience high quality without linking to unapproved content. Phase 2 will add affiliate links to premium instructionals (BJJ Fanatics, MGinAction, Keenan Online) for techniques where no free content exists.

---

## Belt-Level Filtering

**Decision**: Yes, filter by belt level with soft boundaries.

- **White belts**: See White and White/Blue videos. Advanced videos hidden (not shown in recommendations).
- **Blue belts**: See all White through Blue videos. Purple+ videos labeled "Advanced" but accessible.
- **Purple+ belts**: See everything. No restrictions.

Rationale: A white belt doesn't need to see inside heel hook tutorials. Showing them would be confusing and potentially dangerous. But a blue belt curious about leg locks should be able to access purple-level content if they seek it out -- just not proactively recommended.
